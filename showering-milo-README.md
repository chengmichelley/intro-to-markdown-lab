# Milo's Monthly Bathtime Regime 
![photo of Milo in the shower](./Milo%20in%20Shower.jpeg)
This is our step by step process on bathing our 150 pound Alaskan Malamute. 
> Recommend: At least 2 people!
# 1. Bathtime Prep
```
Give Milo anxiety medications 12 hours && 2 hours prior to bathtime 
```
* Pre Bath Brush && Dematting fur 
    * Use detangler and slicker brush
    * Comb out any mats and loose floofs
* Gather Bath & Grooming Essentials
    * Shampoo
    * Ear cleanser
    * Cotton pads
    * Brushes
    * Shower glove
    * Muzzle
    * Shower tether
    * 2 Towels
    * Blow Dryer

> Trick: Bring Milo down during walking time to the dog park. On the way back into building bring him to Dog Spa!

# 2. Bathtime
* Have Milo enter the tub
    * Bring Milo to tub opening and tell him __UP__ command
        * Once he puts his front paws up __LIFT__ his back legs into tub
* Tether Milo to the tub with adjustable nylon collar
    * Prevents him from jumping out
* Adjust temperature of water to a _comfortable_ medium hot 
    * He __DOES NOT LIKE__ cold showers
* Shower Milo to dampen Milo's fur
* Apply _generous_ amount of shampoo all over fur
    * leave head and face area last
* Apply _mist_ & _lather_ until fur is foamy with shampoo
* Rinse out shampoo

> Tip: Milo is anxious in the shower. Have one person talk and rub his head (providing _emotional support_) while the other completes these steps.

# 3. Post Bathtime

* Put ear cleanser on to cotton pad and clean ears
* Let Milo air dry in the tub for a few minutes 
* Towel dry as best as possible 
    * __Focusing__ on _head_ & _face_
* Bring Milo down to drying station
* Tether Milo to drying station
* Cover Milo's ear with a snood to protect & dry ears
* Use two dryers to blow dry Milo
    * Brush coat while using dryer
* Once mostly dry bring Milo upstairs
    * Usually 1.5 hours later
* Towel dry again
* Put dogrobe on Milo 
* ___Give Milo rubs && treats for being a good boy!___
